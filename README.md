# Verisavo Market Simulation

Market Simulation is a Verisavo Decision Application. It lets an organisation explore how a specific market could respond to a decision, under assumptions it can see, question and change.

It is not a forecasting engine. It shows a range of outcomes for each option, the assumptions behind them, which unknowns matter most, and what evidence would narrow the range. The decision stays with the people responsible for it.

## Prototype

`index.html` is a self-contained working prototype. Open it in a browser; it needs no server or install.

Ask any question about an African market. The prototype builds the market around the question, brings the relevant signals into play, and simulates how the market could respond. It then shows the range of outcomes, the assumptions behind them and what is still unknown.

All evidence, figures and sources in the prototype are illustrative.
